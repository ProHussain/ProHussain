## 🚀 What I do

I'm a Product Engineer at **Beyond Apps**, currently building:

- 🎬 **[MyZesty](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.myzesty)** — AI video editor, **500K+ installs**
- 🧞 **Genie** — in development

Over **6 years** I've shipped **50+ production apps** across e-commerce, social, fintech, food delivery, fitness, and utilities. My home stack is native Android; I've also delivered client work in React Native, Flutter, C++, and web — **I own outcomes, not a single stack.**

## 💰 My specialty: apps that earn

Most developers can build features. I build **revenue**:

- 📈 **Ad mediation architecture** — AdMob, AppLovin, IronSource waterfall & bidding setups that have lifted client revenue **25–30%**
- 🧩 **Custom ad SDKs** — remote-config-driven placement systems, A/B-ready
- 🔐 **UMP/GDPR consent flows** — compliant *and* revenue-preserving
- 💳 **IAP & subscriptions** — hybrid revenue models for service and content apps
- 🏪 **Store operations** — launches, ASO, policy compliance across 50+ releases
